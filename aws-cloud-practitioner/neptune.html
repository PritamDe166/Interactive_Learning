AWS CLF-C02 / Databases & analytics / 12 · Domain 3 Technology

Amazon Neptune

  • Neptune is a fully managed graph database.
  • Data is nodes (things) and edges (relationships); queries walk the edges.
  • Best for highly connected data: fraud detection, social networks, recommendations, knowledge graphs.

In terms you know

AWSClosest thing you knowThe difference
Graph queryA recursive CTE joining a table to itself several timesThe graph follows edges directly, so many hops stay fast.

Walkthrough: finding a claims fraud ring

Use Next ▶ to go one step at a time, or Play all

Points to remember

  1. Neptune = managed graph database.
  2. For highly connected data: social networks, fraud detection, recommendation engines, knowledge graphs.
  3. Replicated across 3 AZs; millisecond queries over billions of relationships.
  4. Query languages: Gremlin, openCypher, SPARQL.
  5. Exam trigger: "graph", "relationships between entities".
