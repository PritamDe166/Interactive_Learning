AWS Local Zones

  • Local Zones extend a Region into a city, closer to users there.
  • Run latency-sensitive parts (EC2, EBS, ECS, EKS, some RDS…) with single-digit millisecond latency.
  • Available in many cities worldwide, for example Delhi and Kolkata (parent Region Mumbai).

Walkthrough: bringing compute closer to Delhi

Use Next ▶ to go one step at a time, or Play all

Points to remember

  1. Local Zones = AWS compute, storage, database and other services closer to end users in a metro area.
  2. An extension of a Region (e.g. ap-south-1-del-1 for Delhi).
  3. Single-digit millisecond latency for latency-sensitive applications.
  4. Opt in per zone; supports EC2, EBS, ECS, EKS, some RDS, Direct Connect and more.
  5. Available worldwide, not just in the US.

Which one?

Outposts, Wavelength, Local Zone or a Region?

Cheat-sheet

  • Low latency near a specific city→Local Zone
  • Extension of a Region→Local Zone
