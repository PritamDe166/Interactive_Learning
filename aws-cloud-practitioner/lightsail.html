Amazon Lightsail

  • Lightsail offers virtual servers, databases, storage and networking in one simple console.
  • Low, predictable monthly prices; one-click WordPress, LAMP, Node.js…
  • For users with little cloud experience, simple sites and dev/test.
  • No auto scaling and limited AWS integration: usually a distractor in exam questions.

In terms you know

AWSClosest thing you knowThe difference
LightsailShared or VPS hosting from a web hostSame simplicity, on AWS, with an upgrade path to EC2.

Walkthrough: a WordPress site for a small clinic

Use Next ▶ to go one step at a time, or Play all

Points to remember

  1. Lightsail = simple bundles of servers, storage, databases and networking.
  2. Predictable monthly pricing (from about $5 with IPv4).
  3. Use cases: simple web apps and websites (WordPress, LAMP), dev/test.
  4. High availability possible, no auto scaling, limited AWS integrations.
  5. Exam: correct only for "beginner / simple / predictable price".

Cheat-sheet

  • No cloud experience, simple WordPress site→Lightsail
  • Low, predictable monthly price→Lightsail
  • Needs auto scaling→Not Lightsail: EC2 + ASG
  • Full AWS integration→EC2, RDS, ELB…
