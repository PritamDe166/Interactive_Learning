Three kinds of controls

Inherited

You get them fully from AWS: physical and environmental controls of the data centres. Your auditors can rely on AWS's reports (in AWS Artifact).

Shared

Both sides do their own layer: patch management (AWS patches infrastructure, you patch your guest OS and apps), configuration management, and awareness & training.

Customer-specific

Only you can do them: for example service and communications protection or zone security, routing data within your own security environments.

Merged

Your notes had a separate shared-responsibility page in six sections (Overview, IAM, EC2, EC2 storage, S3, Databases). They're combined here, with one consistent set of duties.

Added

Storage specifics: AWS replicates EBS volumes inside their AZ and EFS data across AZs, replaces faulty hardware, and ensures its staff can't read your data. You set up snapshots and backups, choose encryption, and accept that EC2 Instance Store data is lost when the instance stops.

Added

Exam guide wording (Task 2.1): "how the responsibilities shift depending on the service". The classic contrast: on EC2 you patch the OS; on RDS and Lambda AWS does.

Check yourself

Answer out loud before you open each card.

Who patches the guest operating system on an EC2 instance?Show answer

You. AWS patches the host and hypervisor underneath.

Who patches the database engine on Amazon RDS?Show answer

AWS, in your chosen maintenance window. You still own the schema, data, users and security groups.

An S3 bucket was made public by mistake and data leaked. Whose responsibility?Show answer

The customer's. Bucket policies and Block Public Access settings are configuration in the cloud.

Name one inherited control.Show answer

Physical and environmental controls of AWS data centres.

Is IAM configuration (users, MFA, least privilege) shared?Show answer

No, it's yours. AWS runs the IAM service and enforces your policies, but you decide who gets what and turn on MFA.

Why does Lambda leave you fewer duties than EC2?Show answer

It's more abstracted: AWS runs the servers, OS and language runtime. You own the code, its execution role, and the data it touches.
