AWS CloudShell

  • CloudShell is a terminal inside the AWS console, with the AWS CLI already installed.
  • It runs as the identity you signed in with: no access keys to set up, and no extra permissions either.
  • It is Region-aware: commands default to the console's Region, and your 1 GB home folder is separate per Region.
  • Handy for labs and quick checks. The CLF-C02 exam guide lists it as out of scope.

Points to remember

  1. Browser-based shell, opened from the console toolbar; AWS CLI v2 preinstalled.
  2. Uses your console identity's credentials; IAM permissions still apply. aws sts get-caller-identity shows who you are.
  3. Region-aware: starts in the console's Region; use --region for others. Not available in every Region.
  4. 1 GB persistent $HOME per Region, kept for 120 days after last use. Files outside $HOME don't persist; VPC environments have no persistent storage.
  5. Upload and download files from the Actions menu; tabs and split panes are supported.
  6. Limits: about 10 concurrent shells per Region, sessions end after inactivity and after about 12 hours. Not for production jobs or secrets.
  7. Exam: out of scope for CLF-C02 (listed with developer tools). Learn it as a lab convenience.
