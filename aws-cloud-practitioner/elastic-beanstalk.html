AWS Elastic Beanstalk

  • Elastic Beanstalk is PaaS: you upload code; it builds and runs the infrastructure.
  • It creates EC2, a load balancer, an Auto Scaling group and monitoring (via CloudFormation).
  • Supports .NET, Java, Node.js, Python, PHP, Ruby, Go and Docker.
  • Free itself; you pay for the underlying resources.

In terms you know

AWSClosest thing you knowThe difference
Elastic BeanstalkAzure App Service, or "Web Deploy and forget the servers"The EC2 instances still exist in your account and you can see them.

Points to remember

  1. Beanstalk = PaaS: developer-centric way to deploy web apps.
  2. Handles capacity provisioning, load balancing, auto scaling, health monitoring.
  3. Platforms: .NET, Java, Node.js, Python, PHP, Ruby, Go, Docker.
  4. Uses CloudFormation underneath; you can still access the resources.
  5. No extra charge; pay for EC2, ELB, etc.
  6. Responsibility: you own the code; AWS manages the platform.
