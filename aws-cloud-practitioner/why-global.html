AWS CLF-C02 / Global infrastructure / 1 · Domain 3 Technology

Why Go Global

  • Distance adds latency: users far from your Region wait longer for every request.
  • Deploying in several Regions cuts latency and gives disaster recovery.
  • Edge locations speed things up without full deployments.
  • Building blocks: Regions, AZs, edge locations, Local Zones, Wavelength, Outposts.

Walkthrough: serving patients on four continents

Use Next ▶ to go one step at a time, or Play all

Points to remember

  1. A global application is deployed in several geographies (Regions, edge locations).
  2. Benefits: lower latency, disaster recovery, attack resilience.
  3. Regions and AZs: full deployments. Edge locations: caching (CloudFront) and fast entry to the AWS network.
  4. Services: Route 53, CloudFront, S3 Transfer Acceleration, Global Accelerator, Local Zones, Wavelength, Outposts.

Cheat-sheet

  • Lower latency for distant users→Deploy in more Regions / use the edge
  • Survive a Region failure→Multi-Region (DR)
  • Cache content near users→CloudFront at edge locations
